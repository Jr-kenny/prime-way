The app, in your hands

This phone is live. Tap through a deposit and a real send. Contacts only appear when you send.

How a send flows

Seven small steps.

  1. 1
    Welcome and home. Big USD balance card on top, Deposit and Send under it, then your Assets.
  2. +
    Deposit. Your code, your address, Share and Cross-chain.
  3. 2
    Send to. Paste, Address Book or Scan, then tap a person and Continue.
  4. 3
    Pick their network. Type two letters and it is there.
  5. 4
    Type the amount, in dollars. Pick which asset pays. They get USDC unless you change it. Try DAI and watch it say no.
  6. +
    Settings and Security. Address Book and Security each open their own screen. Inside Security: Add wallet, Export wallet, PIN for sending.
  7. 5
    Check and confirm. Who, how much, what they get. One tap.
  8. 6
    Watch it travel. You see it go, and change into their money on the way.
  9. 7
    “She's got it.”

Meet Pip, the fox

Pip is quick, calm and hard to fluster. Like sending money should feel.

Why a fox? Foxes know every path, even the ones nobody else can see. When there is no straight way there, Pip finds a way round: resourceful, warm, and calm when something goes wrong.

Pip shows up at the moments that matter and stays out of the way the rest of the time. Flat shapes, plain words, never childish.

The converting moment

Your money and their money are not the same kind. On the way, yours becomes theirs, and Prime Way shows it happen.

What the user sees

Mid-journey, the travelling coin ripples and changes colour. Gold becomes teal, B becomes $, and the line under it says “Making it just right for Sarah…”

No rates, no pairs, no charts.

Why it works

The amount screen already promised the number: “Sarah will receive 99.60 USDC.” Pick something else and the app checks Relay can deliver it first. If it cannot, it says so before any money moves.

If the way there needs a stop in the middle, the coin keeps travelling. The path bends; the promise does not change.

The words we never use

Swap. Exchange rate. Pair. Route. Gas. None of them show. The app says sending, on its way, making it just right, and she's got it.
